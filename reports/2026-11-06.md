## 🤖 AI

**1. OpenAI releases GPT-6 Cyber, its highest-risk cybersecurity model yet**

**2. Anthropic launches Claude Personal Finance, linking bank accounts via Plaid**
Anthropic introduced a beta feature letting paid US users link bank and card accounts so Claude can analyze spending; the company says access is read-only and the tool is not designed to give investment advice. *Source: TechCrunch ([link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/claude-can-now-analyze-your-spending-with-its-new-personal-finance-tool/))*

**4. Anthropic eases Claude Code's game-cloning restrictions after developer backlash**
